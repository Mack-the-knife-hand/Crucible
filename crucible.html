What does the AI receive?

Your message and up to 30 recent chat messages; passages on the table with their change-pool notes and nearby text; the outline and opening 1,500 characters, or the whole manuscript when selected.

Enabled story-memory notes and selected images are included when Use story memory is checked, subject to its size limit. Margin notes are not sent automatically. Beta reader and Prose pass use their own selected scope.

AI is optional. Writing, organizing and exporting do not require an API key.
